## Overall Impression

Polished and voice-driven on desktop; conversion plumbing (form, mobile, dead controls) is where the craft promise breaks. Biggest opportunity: 390px experience + inquiry form.

## What's Working

1. Authored brand voice and system (tokens, mono labels, 好 stamp).
2. Evidence density (named clients, metrics, testimonials 1:1 with cases).
3. Conversion clarity (one goal, anchor CTAs, friction reducers at the ask).

## Priority Issues

- **P1 — Mobile horizontal overflow at 390px.** Fixed 54px `text-display` H1 with no step-down. Fix: responsive type (mobile token/clamp) + 390px overflow sweep. Command: $impeccable adapt
- **P1 — No mobile navigation.** `hidden md:flex`, no hamburger, CTA `hidden sm:`. Fix: hamburger + mobile menu with links + CTA. Command: $impeccable adapt
- **P1 — Inquiry form is conversion-fragile.** No `<form>`, `type="button"`, message-less validation, unchecked name/email, no aria-live confirm. Fix: real form, Enter submit, inline errors, email check, live confirmation. Command: $impeccable harden
- **P2 — Dead controls erode the craft claim.** `href="#"` nav, handler-less newsletter Join, inert person icon. Fix: wire, link, or remove each. Command: $impeccable polish
- **P2 — Persuasion architecture.** Estimator doesn't estimate; proof after ask; dead-footer ending; identical case rhythms; nested cards. Fix: compute or rename, proof before ask, CTA close, vary rhythm, flatten cards. Command: $impeccable layout
